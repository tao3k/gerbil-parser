#+title: LR construction lifecycle: paper, source and model

* Paper and implementation boundary

The closure and goto definitions in [[https://www.deinprogramm.de/sperber/papers/lr-essence-pepm.pdf][The Essence of LR Parsing]], Section 2.2,
are the semantic target. The implementation uses raw production records,
a dense core-symbol vector, interned symbol IDs and sparse bit masks. This
composition derives group meaning and layout bounds from those representations;
it does not require a final group-correctness certificate.

* Constructed representation

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/PhysicalOperandExtraction.lean][PhysicalOperandExtraction]] projects actual operand base fields, discarding
annotations and actions. Induction over the native vector-write recurrence
proves every bounded read, including the untouched completed slot, equals the
constructed grammar's after-dot symbol. A fresh zero-filled vector is required.
[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/PhysicalCoreExtraction.lean][PhysicalCoreExtraction]] inverts arbitrary physical addresses and proves that
a symbol-bearing core advances to address plus one inside the same production.
Duplicate semantic productions retain separate physical addresses.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/PhysicalSymbolDiscovery.lean][PhysicalSymbolDiscovery]] derives the nonterminal discovery stream from the
physical vector. [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/SymbolDiscovery.lean][SymbolDiscovery]] separately constructs terminal and nonterminal
symbol IDs from an empty registry and proves index injectivity. Completed slots
contribute neither kind of symbol; terminal and nonterminal namespaces remain
distinct even when their numeric payloads agree.

* Sparse groups and publication

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/SparseGroupMasks.lean][SparseGroupMasks]] follows accumulation, own/shared mask union and the
skip/merge recurrence. Its membership theorem identifies the origin of every
output bit in either the kernel or prediction items. [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/SparseGoto.lean][SparseGoto]] combines this
origin with physical symbol discovery and bounded advance. Every returned mask
is bounded without a caller-supplied bound; every decoded target is exactly a
paper goto-kernel item from the generated closure.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/KernelConstruction.lean][KernelConstruction]] starts at augmented physical core zero and derives
bounds for every successor in its construction history. Composing group meaning,
registry publication and the concrete closure factory gives the target closure
of each returned symbol/target pair. The theorem quantifies over arbitrary
grammars, histories, masks and published groups, rather than a fixture catalogue.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/SparseGroupSource.lean][SparseGroupSource]] independently describes the complete native symbol-catalog
and sparse-group declarations. The existing source extractor generates both
witnesses, and Lean rejects a declaration mismatch. Its checked adapter exposes
the proved mathematical group implementation only after schema equality.
Source equality identifies the supported procedure; it alone is not a proof of
arbitrary Scheme execution or of all mutable primitive behavior.

* One construction contract

The paper-level model entry is [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionProtocol.lean][ConstructionProtocol.construct]]. It starts at the
augmented dot-zero kernel and returns registry validity, complete published-row
ownership and an exhausted queue. Consumers use =complete= and =paper_target=;
they do not assemble local certificates for an arbitrary final table.

#+end_example

This is a maintained contract map, not a diary of proof additions. A producer
change must preserve its output law and revalidate the named consumer. The
existing source generator checks complete declarations, the axiom audit imports
the public entry, and model-input discovery binds the native fixtures and this
topic to qualification. A theorem or an added file alone does not close a row.

* Sorting and borrowed storage

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/DiscoveryOrder.lean][DiscoveryOrder]] proves first-occurrence order for arbitrary sorted physical
core streams and repeated symbols. [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/GroupOrder.lean][GroupOrder]] derives template order from
production offsets, proves the actual accumulator/skip/merge order and shows
that equal earliest addresses identify equal rows. Any sort with the ordinary
sorted-permutation contract therefore gives the same own rows; stability and
a particular sorting algorithm are unnecessary.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/GroupScratch.lean][GroupScratch]] follows the native zero-test, first-slot write, bit insertion,
touched-list insertion and cleanup. The touched list is a permutation of the
constructed keys. Every selected first slot is initialized before use and then
retained. Cleanup restores every mask to zero while leaving first slots stale.
=native_group_call= combines these mutable facts with sorting and sparse merge,
proving equality with the group producer consumed by the goto proof.

* Complete queue and publication

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionQueue.lean][ConstructionQueue]] models dequeue before discovery, processed-count
increment and interning/enqueue of novel kernels. The FIFO invariant identifies
pending IDs with the exact unprocessed interval. The finite physical kernel
universe minus processed states strictly decreases each nonempty iteration.
This is a termination bound, not a practical estimate, timeout or test fuel.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionRows.lean][ConstructionRows]] proves that each processed ID owns a complete row.
Later interning preserves source and target IDs. Exhaustion publishes every
discovered state. =paper_target= composes the final table with generated
prediction, physical extraction and paper goto/closure for every final edge.

* Ordered bit traversal and LeanPoo consumption

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/BitTraversal.lean][BitTraversal]] proves that =rest & (rest - 1)= removes exactly the least
selected bit, including arbitrary-precision borrow chains. The direct loop
terminates and produces a strictly ascending list with exact bit membership.
The field loop finishes each local traversal before advancing its offset. Empty
fields and a partial final field preserve that order. Aligned start and integer
length bounds derive the same complete callback list as the direct loop for
any positive field width. Population-count accuracy affects branch selection,
but either selected branch has the same callback sequence.

The relational =DirectRun= and =FieldRun= rules describe recursive source
control flow without assuming final sortedness. Their soundness laws derive
the sequence above. [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/BitTraversalSource.lean][BitTraversalSource]] independently checks the complete
traversal declaration and the SDK-derived field-width binding, including the
fixnum loop, direct loop, field visitor and branch arithmetic. Reordering the
callback and clear operations or changing offset advancement invalidates the
source witness. The reader handles both procedure and value declarations.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/TraversalCapabilities.lean][TraversalCapabilities]] reuses the pinned LeanPoo APIs instead of defining
another family/certificate mechanism:

- =Requirements.certify= retains direct, field and selected factories with a
  joint equality contract at the exact mask/width context.
- =Certified.entails= derives ordered callbacks while retaining those factories.
- =Certified.project= narrows the family to the selected capability.
- =Certified.consume= constructs the proof-aware callback interface. It applies
  the selected factory, not every strategy in the broad proof family.

Pure integer/loop proofs remain mathematical functions. Capability retention,
contract consequence, projection and consumption use LeanPoo. Existing parser
preparation also uses LeanPoo Requirements and lookahead uses DictionaryBridge;
this adds the same contract discipline to construction traversal.

=callback_cores= derives equality with the grammar's physical =coreItems=.
=traversedGroups_exact= substitutes the selected callback sequence into scratch
accumulation, and =traversal_tick_exact= connects that producer to append/enqueue
and publication. Finally, =traversal_finish_exact= proves equality of the entire
terminating queue drain with the canonical construction. Classification and
population are functions of each mask, so a run may switch traversal paths
between states. This is a consumer
connection, not a certificate attached to an unused traversal implementation.

The external primitive boundary states the ordinary meanings of first-set-bit,
integer-length, extraction, bitwise arithmetic and a positive SDK field width.
=least_unique= relates a primitive satisfying the least-bit specification to
the model. Source execution explicitly requires zero to classify as a fixnum,
so it never admits a nonzero-path first-set-bit invocation on an empty mask.
It neither inspects nor rebuilds Gambit. Normal callback order can
be consumed by any order-sensitive fold; native controls separately exercise
nested callbacks and immediate exception propagation.

* Cache ownership in the unified construction

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/CachedConstruction.lean][CachedConstruction.construct]] executes the combined construction model.
It threads one initially empty grammar-owned cache through initial installation,
novel kernel interning, ordered transition publication and the terminating FIFO
drain. It also consumes the selected LeanPoo traversal capability in each group
call. =construct_exact= identifies the whole resulting machine with
=ConstructionProtocol.construct=, so its complete-row and paper goto/closure
contracts apply without a supplied final-state semantic certificate.

The cache key is the aggregate prediction mask, not the kernel mask or state ID.
[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/TemplateCapabilities.lean][TemplateCapabilities]] retains the fetching factory through LeanPoo
=Requirements.certify=. Its joint contract includes both exact template data and
validity of the next cache. =Certified.consume= uses those data to build the
kernel-specific closure and next cache. Hits share predictions, never the complete
closure of distinct kernels. The grammar indexes the retained factory/cache;
there is no cross-grammar reuse or label-only admission.

=build_stored= proves installation at the requested aggregate key and
=build_retained= preserves earlier entries. =construct_covered= carries cache
coverage through the complete queue. [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/PlanStorage.lean][PlanStorage]] now gives the retained data
an explicit representation: a directory maps aggregate masks to logical object
references; objects hold immutable templates; physical plan slots contain
=(kernel, reference)= pairs. A miss allocates the next fresh reference, while a
hit retains its reference. =fresh_separates= and =objects_retained= exclude writes
to previously reachable objects. =shared_reference= proves that equal prediction
keys in different plan slots refer to the same object.

=decode_install= derives a new plan's decoded template. =decode_other= proves
that installing another plan preserves older pair/reference reads, including
when allocation occurs. The bounded =vectorize= producer and =vector_decode=
connect plan slots to vector indices. [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/VectorStorage.lean][VectorStorage]] additionally executes the
ascending native copy recurrence into fresh false slots. =copy_read= proves copy
coverage and untouched initialization; =grow_exact= identifies its result with
the prefix-preserving growth specification. =append_refines= then derives the
captured-ID write and retention of every older allocated slot. It applies to
state, transition and plan values, rather than one selected fixture type.

=PlanStorage.Trace= starts before state zero, with the admitted positive initial capacity.
Each novel kernel advances the captured count once and executes growth only at
capacity. =trace_shape= derives positive capacity, the count bound, physical
length and bounded read correspondence. =intern_physical=, =publish_physical=
and =finish_physical= carry that actual vector witness alongside the same memory
through the entire construction. =constructed_vector= returns the final vector
and every allocated plan's decoded value without a supplied capacity or
final-vector certificate. The addresses are logical identities, not numeric claims
about host pointers or GC placement.

The LeanPoo factory now constructs that memory operation. Its joint certificate
contains exact template data, validity of the next memory and the exact install
operation. =Certified.consume= uses the memory validity evidence to construct the
next owned cache. =construct_aligned= derives agreement of every installed plan
with the canonical grammar factory through initial installation, every target
append and the complete queue. The queue reads =Cache.readTemplate= by following
the physical plan reference and supplies the returned template directly to
=runGroup=. It does not substitute a key-catalog read or rematerialize a template.

The same owned cache carries scratch masks, firsts and touched order. =runGroup=
scans that actual preceding state and resets its touched masks before returning
it. =tick_scratch= proves that subsequent target installations and publication
preserve precisely that returned scratch; =publish_scratch= derives the frame
law for an arbitrary number of targets. Firsts remain stale by design, but their
values now come from the preceding scan rather than an externally supplied
function. =runGroup_memory= proves the converse frame: scratch operations retain
all template objects and physical plan slots. The source's fresh vectors and
immutable template layout justify their separate model fields; host allocation
and alias encoding remain explicit premises, checked by native identity controls.

The native three-slot template also has a group hash index.
[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/TemplateIndex.lean][TemplateIndex]] models first-row creation, table insertion and subsequent
mask updates. =record_view= relates each write to the existing accumulation
state; induction gives =lookup= for every key, including absent entries and
repeated symbols. The materializer now builds that index, and =runGroup= uses
it for shared-row lookup. =own_exact= substitutes the constructed index into
ordered sparse grouping. There is no caller-supplied final-index certificate.
Physical hash equality, row-address encoding and primitive mutation remain
external; the index producer's control flow and query meaning are proved.

** Source extraction and executable recipes

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionExtraction.lean][ConstructionExtraction]] reads allocation parameters from named lexical
bindings in the generated reader AST. It rejects absent or duplicate bindings,
requires the three initial capacities to agree and separately requires all three
expansion factors to agree. Full independent builder, growth, append and template
schemas then bind those scalar values to the copy loop and publication order.
=extracted_sources= proves this complete correspondence for any accepted source.
The current reader-derived recipe has initial capacity 128 and factor two.
The same constructor accepts every positive initial capacity through
=Recipe.layout=; =PlanStorage.empty= and the complete queue use that value.

Recipes initialize a vector and execute the proved copy/write protocol;
=recipe_write= and =recipe_copy= apply to arbitrary bounded append requests.
The source check uses equation rewriting and existing complete-schema theorems,
not native decision or a source-text checksum. A source change to a scalar,
copy destination, publication order or template update must pass extraction and
all corresponding proof obligations again. The decoder admits the supported
construction idiom; it does not assign semantics to arbitrary Scheme programs.

LeanPoo's =ContextSlot= is deliberately not used: it retains only one context,
whereas this builder retains multiple aggregate masks. =ConstructionCache=
materializes dependency graphs; the LR prediction dependency graph has already
been completed before this cache is constructed. Reusing either with a different
lifetime or key protocol would misrepresent the source. Capability retention,
joint certification and proof-aware consumption reuse LeanPoo directly; mask
arithmetic and FIFO induction remain ordinary functions and proofs.

** Source-admitted complete observations

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionConsumer.lean][ConstructionConsumer]] is the source-admitted entry for the retained complete
construction. It runs =prepare= before the LeanPoo construction factory and
consumes the joint machine, alignment and three-vector history evidence once.
Rejected sources cannot execute the factory. Successful construction retains
one completed cache; observations never accept an independent final index or
vector certificate.

=joint_observations= exposes the actual heap threaded through the complete
queue, not three vectors reconstructed from its final logical table.
[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionStorage.lean][ConstructionStorage]] uses one counted copy loop: state, transition row,
then plan at each ascending index. =copy_fields= derives the projections of that
loop; =append_exact= proves synchronized expansion and captured-ID writes.
Transitions are copied without being overwritten by state installation.
=publish_refines= separately accounts for each row publication.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionHeap.lean][ConstructionHeap]] transports one =Owned= contract through initial installation,
interning, each group publication and the terminating queue. =construct_owned=
derives both a joint operational history and the three read correspondences.
The retained LeanPoo factory supplies that evidence to the complete consumer.
=joint_index= queries the shared table reached through the actual plan vector,
including absent keys. =physical_complete= gives every allocated node a row
in the actual transition vector; =physical_paper_target= connects such rows and
the physical target-state vector to paper goto/closure semantics.

The source-procedure test helper permits an explicit AST transform. The layout
fixture changes exactly the three named initial allocation literals, retaining
all other source instructions and initializers. The new independent observer
forwards every allocation and write unchanged, records the actual copy order,
and compares complete results with the ordinary source constructor. Negative
controls change only captured receipts: missing copies, wrong indices and wrong
values must be rejected. The existing published-index corruption control is
retained. No runtime fixture substitutes a copied LR construction algorithm.

** Source control and the joint history

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionControl.lean][ConstructionControl]] audits the boundary between the joint append/publication
history and actual =grow!= control. =decode= recognizes the entire supported
declaration: the full-capacity guard, each allocation initializer, loop start
zero, strict count bound, unit increment, matching copy sources/destinations
and three ordered owner assignments. It returns an executable program derived
from these instructions. Unsupported or malformed programs cannot execute via
=executeSource=; a nonadvancing loop is rejected rather than translated to +1.

The interpreter uses the native forward tail-recursive cursor. =forward_prefix=
proves its equivalence to the counted joint copy for arbitrary heaps and bounds.
=loop_safe= and =growth_safe= derive valid source and target accesses at every
iteration from the owned shape. Native bounds safety is not inferred from the
model's out-of-range list-set behavior. =owner_stages= makes intermediate owners
explicit: after the first assignment, states are new while rows and plans are
old; after the second, only plans remain old. The invariant requiring equal
capacities applies again after all three assignments complete.

Independent =let= allocation initializers need no extra order premise at the
value representation: =allocation_permutation= proves their commutation.
Fresh/disjoint physical allocation and address encoding remain host contracts.
The model does not claim that allocation traces or physical addresses agree
under different evaluation orders.

=trace_realizes= remains a joint-history refinement tool. The retained LeanPoo
factory now executes [[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionFIFO.lean][ConstructionFIFO.build]], which generates the exact actions
during initialization and FIFO execution. Its joint contract certifies those
actions, their safety and replay together with the same machine, cache and
owned heap. =source_execution= reaches the generating factory, while
=source_replay= uses the retained actions rather than an unrelated existential
history. No final-heap certificate or execution trace is supplied by a caller.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionAppend.lean][ConstructionAppend]] decodes the complete supported =append-state!= declaration:
growth, capture of the old index, template lookup binding, state write, plan
write, table registration, counter increment and return of the captured index.
The executable frame keeps its counter, captured index, registration effects
and return value distinct. =registration_stage= proves that both vector writes
are visible while registration still observes the old counter. =run_exact=
derives the new counter and returned old ID; =write_bounds= proves valid writes
for every owned shape. =intern_step= supplies the actual retained producer's
closure and shared template reference to that execution. =execute_exact= and
=trace_realizes= lift the instruction protocol through every complete joint
history. Source admission checks growth and append declarations before replay.
=intern_index= replays the registration effects into the prior logical table
and proves equality with the producer's resulting index function at every key;
registration is not merely an emitted event label.

Native controls execute the actual append declaration with forwarding writes
and passive snapshots. Capacities 1, 2, 3, 5 and 128 exercise count zero, the
last free slot and growth. They verify template/closure callback identity,
state/plan write order, registration before counter advancement and return of
the old ID. Missing, reversed and premature-counter receipts are rejected.
The joint Quint model also separates state write, plan write, registration,
counter advancement and return; random simulation uses these microsteps.

** Initialization and FIFO action generation

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionInitialization.lean][ConstructionInitialization]] decodes the initial writes and registration
separately from =append-state!=. Root counters must be one and zero, slot-zero
state and plan writes precede registration, and there is no initial increment
or growth call. =run_exact= relates this special path to the normal-form first
append; =write_bounds= uses the extracted positive capacity. The actual initial
cache heap is produced by those instructions, and the initial index is produced
by replaying their registration effects. =initial_exact= leaves the queue empty;
the outer queue alone enqueues ID zero in =start_exact=.

=ConstructionFIFO.decode= checks the outer empty test, seed ID, dequeue,
processed-count increment, plan read, ordered target callback, reverse/write
publication and tail call. Target lookup preserves ID zero as an existing
value. The effect interpreter separates installation, accumulator cons and
conditional enqueue. =target_exact= and =groups_exact= prove equivalence to the
retained producer, including cache and reversed accumulator, for arbitrary
target lists. During a fresh append, one allocated ID is temporarily absent
from the queue; the callback restores the stable accounting only after cons
and enqueue. This legal intermediate state must not be mistaken for a broken
stable FIFO invariant.

=tick_exact= and =finish_exact= connect interpreted dequeue/publication to the
complete cached construction. The loop terminates by the existing finite-core
capacity minus processed count, without fuel or a final-state premise. The same
execution emits its actions. =groups_replay=, =tick_replay= and =finish_replay=
compose their heap effects; =construct_replay= normalizes the special initial
path and reaches the actual returned heap and count.

[[file:../../../formal/ParserEngine/ConstructionFIFOTrace.lean][ConstructionFIFOTrace]] proves safety of these exact generated actions.
Owned shapes provide append/growth bounds; queued-node validity and append-only
allocation provide every publication bound. =construct_safe= is generated
through the same terminating loop, not borrowed from some equal final heap.
LeanPoo consumes this safe action stream, replay and producer identity.
=ConstructionConsumer.source_complete= combines source execution, exact-action
safety, replay and physical row validity; =physical_paper_target= connects the
same retained row and target vectors to the existing paper closure/goto meaning.

The execution entry extracts its own layout from all admitted source schemas.
A caller cannot pass an unrelated positive capacity. Malformed admission returns
before construction. Normal-return helper algorithms retain their separately
proved meanings; diagnostic I/O, primitive operations, compiler execution and
nonlocal control behavior remain external contracts.

Passive native controls read and execute the real builder at capacities 1, 3
and 128. They check initialization before seeding, dequeue order, per-target
counter values, row-before-enqueue order, reuse without enqueue and exactly one
reversed row publication. Receipt mutations reject missing seed and wrong row
owners. A source-extracted target callback separately verifies that an existing
ID zero calls neither append nor enqueue. The dedicated Quint source protocol
checks deferred enqueue, duplicate target reuse and reversed-row publication.

This is a refinement of the parser-owned vector-effect protocol on normal
return. The decoded certified queue generates append and row actions;
hash/queue primitives and the producer callbacks retain their existing
contracts. It is not a small-step semantics for arbitrary Scheme, a compiler
correctness theorem, or an exception/continuation refinement through primitive
calls. Those host behaviors remain external rather than silently discharged by
source equality or finite tests.

The passive native control observes the actual nested growth body. It records
the copy count at each lexical owner assignment and retains all three owner
references. Full copies must precede every owner switch; the intermediate old
references must match the source. Reversed, missing and premature owner receipts
are rejected, including receipts whose final vector contents would still agree.

The composition qualification passes 190 Lean build jobs with only the standard
=propext=, =Classical.choice= and =Quot.sound= axioms in the new audited theorems.
The existing Scheme test macros run 281 cases across 13 owners, including final
physical queries, joint copies at capacities 1, 2, 3, 5 and 128, repeated growth,
recursive shared-template growth, receipt mutations, nonpositive-capacity
rejection, passive owner-control observations and the damaged-index control.
The suite retains unchanged 90-second total and 5-second idle limits. Fresh
source witnesses and 19 evidence controls pass. The joint-heap Quint model has
eleven directed controls and 1,000 bounded
histories of 60 transitions, including staging copies, publication of copied
rows, non-power-of-two growth, partially switched owners, wrong owner order and
premature publication, early counter and wrong return rejection. The existing
complete lifecycle remains a separate bounded check. These finite checks
support the universal Lean composition and do not replace it. No CPU improvement
is claimed.
The source FIFO model additionally passes ten directed checks and 1,000
histories of 80 transitions, including wrong initial counter, missing seed,
duplicate existing enqueue, missing fresh enqueue and missing reverse controls.
Its dequeue and processed-counter actions are separate, including the legal
transient state before accounting catches up with the removed queue head.

These conditions are acceptance boundaries, not obligations silently discharged
by finite tests. =current_admitted= admits the current source; =prepared= admits
every successfully extracted recipe. Capacity no longer needs a separate 128
guard. =schema_recipe= proves extraction for every positive capacity of the
supported complete schema; =schema_constructs= produces a complete certified
result whose executed layout has exactly that extracted capacity. The external
conditions still include primitive arithmetic, storage,
allocation, fresh/disjoint ownership, hashing, queue operations and ordinary
sorted-permutation behavior. Arbitrary Scheme execution, compiler lowering,
GC and continuation/exception behavior inside host primitives remain external.
No engine optimization is justified by this composition alone.

This table is the integration checklist. Adding an isolated theorem does not
close a row until the complete constructor consumes its evidence and a native
