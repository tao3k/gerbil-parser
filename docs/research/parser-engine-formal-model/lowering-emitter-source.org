#+title: Shared-heap source emission and retained construction

* Contract and paper target

The target remains the paper-level grammar derivation and LR construction in
[[file:lowering-publication.org][the publication contract]]. A dense sequence of production identifiers is only
one representation obligation. It does not establish grammar meaning, AST
semantics or the correctness of an arbitrary source dispatcher.

The source path is the actual =lower-to= local closure, =emit-production=,
=make-production=, counter assignment and reverse-list insertion in
[[file:../../../src/compiler/lr.ss][the parser implementation]]. The constructor is a five-field Scheme list.
The model executes that declaration; it does not replace it with an opaque
record primitive or assume its result agrees with an expected record.

| Existing retained product | =LoweringEmissionConstruction.retained_trace_ids= | Source-generated IDs equal the records of the same successful LeanPoo construction product |

The mixed trace contains actual =Eval= and =Invoke= derivations. It does not
accept caller-provided final heap equalities, frame assertions or a guessed
counter-to-record alignment certificate. Determinism connects each returning
source call to its constructed execution witness. Fresh-name search terminates
under the existing finite reservation and injective candidate contracts.

The root storage layout owns the production counter and publication list;
helper allocation owns different cells. The declaration collector is allocated
before that root. Their separation follows from the same allocator contract,
not independent nonaliasing assertions for every branch. The fixed-argument
heap-write commutation theorem is a frame law, not permission to reorder
source-dependent operations or a concurrency/weak-memory claim.

The existing LeanPoo construction factory remains the consumer. The bridge
relates physical IDs to its retained dependent product; its explicit payload
encoder is not a proof of arbitrary raw grammar normalization or complete
payload decoding. No second automaton or certification factory is introduced.

* Gaps found by the source audit

The earlier dispatch theorem accepted selected-branch execution while the
common emitter had no source evaluation proof. The constructor, captured local
call and two publication writes are now derived for the unobserved profile.
The arbitrary mixed-trace theorem prevents silently resetting the production
counter between helper and parent emissions, or confusing it with the helper
name counter. Native controls exercise existing prefixes and caller shadowing.

The signed scalar extraction defect is addressed in
[[file:lowering-observation-and-metadata.org][recursive effects, observation and metadata]]. That audit also executes the
observed local context/root emitter chain and records its remaining callback
capability and full recursive-dispatch obligations.

* Remaining simulation obligations

- Generate the mixed operational trace from every complete recursive Scheme
  branch, including recursive procedure calls, instead of assuming that trace.
- Derive parallel precedence bindings and native field/alias action execution.
- Generate observed paths, choice/recurrence proof recipes and admitted
  callback authority against the source-executed context/emitter chain.
- Connect arbitrary admitted raw grammar payloads and the full concrete record
  encoding to the retained product; signed scalar transport now has its own
  universal encoding and constructor laws.

The proofs here concern normal returns with both observers disabled. They do
not establish rollback on allocation failure, exceptions, continuation reentry
or arbitrary observer mutation. Installed reader, arithmetic, list, ordered-map,
compiler and GC behavior retain their explicit host contracts. Gambit and the
SDK are neither modified nor built. Local parser CPU optimization remains
blocked by the unified model frontier.
