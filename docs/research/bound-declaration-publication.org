#+title: Ordered declaration origins and complete Bound IR publication
#+startup: overview

* One compilation responsibility

This implements the publication part of the
[[file:scheme-component-provenance.org][component provenance study]] and
[[file:scheme-source-origin-contract.org][reader-origin contract]]. The original producer is =5bc7d2f=;
the integrated parent =4319c94= retains its source and adds shared CPU sampling.
The language author interfaces remain =grammar.ss=, =parser.ss= and
=parser-test.ss=. No new author composition clause, callback or IR schema is
introduced.

The user-selected
[[https://github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/blob/f84155662b57ec8034bb60f0d14968715ad15cce/docs/scheme-coding-style.org][Scheme Coding Style]] guides ownership, representation, admission and evidence.
The relevant upstream mechanisms are Gerbil =2591dcd='s
=std/list/list-builder.ss= and =gerbil/expander/stx.ss=. The former owns fresh
queue cells through hygienic syntax; the latter provides =stx-for-each= and
defines =syntax->list= as =stx-map values=. Source syntax can therefore be
visited directly without first copying an identity-mapped list.

* Paper, contract and implementation

[[https://www2.ccs.neu.edu/racket/pubs/icfp10-cf.pdf][Ryan Culpepper and Matthias Felleisen, Fortifying Macros, ICFP 2010]] argues for
checking macro uses and explaining errors at the author's language boundary.
Our inference is that optimization must preserve the selected declaration,
reader syntax and contextual source record through lowering. The paper does
not prescribe our indexes, canonical digests or list representation. This
change does not implement Racket's =syntax-parse= system.

| Owner | Implemented responsibility | Preserved contract |
|-------+----------------------------+--------------------|
| [[file:../../src/compiler/normalize.ss][POO normalization]] | One ordered composition collector; direct field-origin emission | Parent order, repeated inherited occurrences, role order, cycle rejection and selected source ownership |
| [[file:../../src/compiler/language-artifact.ss][Declaration expansion]] | =stx-for-each= emits field origins directly | Reader syntax identity, field order, scope and malformed-row diagnostics |
| [[file:../../src/compiler/bound-ir.ss][Bound IR publication]] | Invocation-owned source selection; direct section, field and reference construction | First source entries, qualified field names, present false values, declaration/reference order, IDs and canonical digests |

All binding IDs are collected before references are validated, so forward
references remain legal and unresolved references fail before publication.
The required reference list still exists: it is canonical digest input. The
temporary flattened binding catalog, per-kind field batches, reversed bound
sections and variadic reference flattening are removed. Published source records
and declaration payloads are borrowed exactly as before; queue mutation is
confined to new list cells.

Source selection previously repeated linear =assoc= searches for every binding.
One private index per known source namespace now retains original association
entries. This preserves the first row and a present =#f= value; qualified field
keys use structural equality. The index belongs to one binding invocation and
is neither published nor retained by a grammar or parser. With bounded key
shapes and normal hash behavior, source indexing and lookup cost is expected
to grow with source records plus bindings, rather than their product. This
algorithmic analysis is separate from measured complete-publication CPU.

#+begin_src scheme
;; Private publication extent: data order and complete closure share one owner.
(with-list-builder (emit)
  (for-each
   (lambda (section)
     (for-each
      (lambda (binding)
        (table-set! binding-ids (bound-grammar-ir-ref binding 'bindingId) #t)
        (for-each emit (bound-grammar-ir-ref binding 'references)))
      (cdr section)))
   sections))
;; Validate references after every binding ID is present, before publishing IR.
#+end_src

** Question: why does this improve the Scheme DSL boundary?

Author declarations retain their vocabulary and source syntax. POO composition
selects the effective declaration; the compiler owns its transient construction
and lookup costs. Hygienic construction syntax expresses private list ownership,
=syntax-case= retains identifier/context-sensitive admission, and named functions
express closed algorithms. Adding a public generic protocol would not help this
fixed publication contract.

** Question: why is this scientifically testable?

[[file:../../t/bound-ir-index-test.ss][Independent controls]] check first-entry selection, empty first sections,
present false values, qualified fields, defaults, duplicate declaration rejection
and unresolved references. The 20,000-binding case preserves full declaration
order without a variadic call. The original producer rejects that same input
with Gambit's =Number of arguments exceeds implementation limit= at reference
flattening. This is an engine algorithm defect, not a GC diagnosis or a timing
sample. Existing composition, declaration, LR-origin and language ABI consumers
check the entire compiler chain.

[[file:../../t/benchmarks/bound-source-catalog/benchmark.ss][The permanent benchmark]] measures complete binding, closure checks and all
canonical digest construction. Its original constructor is an experiment input,
never a production fallback. Every original/candidate product must equal the
entire current sidecar, including source records and digests. Catalog construction
is outside timing. Existing shared samplers retain natural GC, admitted allocation
populations and alternating CPU order. These are binding-publication measurements,
not complete grammar compilation or parsing throughput.

* Qualification and negative evidence

The completed normalization, syntax-origin and Bound IR chain passed 12 native
consumer modules and all 136 cases in 47.121 seconds, exit zero, final =OK=,
no supervision reason or missing receipt. The original large-catalog control
exited one with the call-arity error; it is not a successful performance receipt.
Optimized native compilation of the two source-origin owners completed in
56.481 seconds. Local reuse of native artifacts remains distinct from clean
platform CI.

An initial CPU experiment used ten-call batches. It completed some component
measurements and two paired populations, then stopped under the unchanged
five-second idle watchdog during the larger catalog. That aggregate run is
rejected. Two-call batches retain the same complete inputs, twenty alternating
groups and the sampler's one-millisecond resolution requirement; they allow
genuine group receipts within the original progress budget. Allocation intervals
that contain GC are excluded rather than replaced with plausible estimates.

The completed two-call CPU comparison used 20 alternating groups per size,
preserved every complete sidecar and passed the one-millisecond batch-resolution
check. It exited zero in 32.655 seconds with final =OK=:

| Syntax kinds (two bindings each) | Ratio P10 | Ratio P50 | Ratio P90 |
|--------------------------------+-----------+-----------+-----------|
| 16 | 0.718 | 1.019 | 1.169 |
| 128 | 0.526 | 1.062 | 1.773 |
| 512 | 0.635 | 0.810 | 1.149 |

Ratios are candidate/original user+system CPU including natural GC. The upper
quantiles and small/medium medians reject a stable speedup claim. The incomplete
ten-call experiment also produced inconsistent ratios: its 16-kind median was
1.413 and its 128-kind median 1.030. Neither population can be discarded merely
because a later median looks better.

Its component allocation population admitted 19 samples on each 16-kind side,
with medians 1,794,904 and 1,712,315.2 bytes/call. This modest observation is not
a large-catalog claim: the original 128-kind population admitted zero samples,
the candidate admitted six, and both 512-kind populations admitted zero. Missing
allocation evidence remains missing. Canonical serialization, digest construction
and retained object effects still need attribution before another performance
claim; no private index result proves full grammar compilation speed.

The saved native comparison entry was then invoked with the original constructor,
20 groups, two calls and 16 kinds. It preserved all complete sidecars and exited
zero in 28.583 seconds with =BOUND-SOURCE-COMPARISON-OK= and final =OK=. Its CPU
ratios were 0.746 / 0.985 / 1.646 (P10/P50/P90), again rejecting stable speedup.
The integrated parent =4319c94= completed Ubuntu Scheme, macOS Scheme and Rust
AOT CI successfully. Those parent checks do not qualify the new source slice;
its pushed revision still needs its own clean-checkout platform results.
